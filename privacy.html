Lumen Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Lumen is a budgeting app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Carson Reinhardt. This policy explains what Lumen does with your information. The short version: your budget stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. Lumen has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and the developer never sees your data.

What Lumen stores

Lumen stores what you enter: your bills, cards, debts, income, categories, purchases, goals, notes and settings. It also keeps the conversation with Luna, the in-app assistant. All of it is stored on your device. Luna runs entirely on your device and doesn't send anything anywhere.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync, Lumen saves your budget to your private iCloud database, so it appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy. The developer can't read it. You can turn sync off at any time, or delete a budget from iCloud, in Lumen's settings.

Sharing a budget

If you share a budget, the people you invite can see it, and can edit it if you allow that. This uses Apple's iCloud sharing. You can stop sharing or remove someone at any time.

Financial data from Apple Wallet

If you allow it, Lumen uses Apple's FinanceKit to read purchases from your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Apple Savings accounts, starting from the day you allow it. From each purchase, Lumen uses only the amount, merchant name, date and account name, so it can add the purchase to your budget. This information is processed on your device. It's stored only in your budget, which syncs through your own iCloud if you turned sync on. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling. Lumen only reads this data and can't move money or change your accounts. You can turn this off in Lumen, or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Financial Data.

Notifications, widgets and Shortcuts

Reminders and alerts are scheduled on your device. Widgets and Shortcuts read your budget on the device through an app group, a storage area shared only between Lumen and its own widgets.

What Lumen doesn't do

  • No sign-up and no account with the developer.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to your bank logins or card numbers.

Deleting your data

You can delete a budget in Lumen's settings, including its copy in iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. Data in iCloud can also be removed under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

Lumen isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
